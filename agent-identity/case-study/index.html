Building Trust in the Age of AI

Setting up identity for a new kind of actor — the AI agent.

The Problem

A New Kind of Actor

Twilio has identity tools for humans — but no way to safely authenticate or authorize the AI agents now acting alongside them.

Example

Owl Trade, a financial tool, wants to allow its users to connect ChatGPT to their Owl Trade account so that ChatGPT can trade on a user's behalf. How do you grant that access — securely, and only as far as it should go?

Who this is for

The End User

Asked, in the moment, whether an agent can act on their behalf.

The Builder

The admin or customer who wants to make their product usable by agents — configuring identity and approvals ahead of time, for their whole platform.

The Solution

An Identity Layer for Agents

A way to grant an AI agent scoped, verifiable, revocable authority — without ever handing over a human's full account.

[ 01 ]

Verifiable identity

Every agent carries a credential that proves who it's acting for.

[ 02 ]

Scoped permission

Access limited to exactly the task it's authorized for — nothing wider.

[ 03 ]

Revocable, auditable

Every action traceable. Every grant reversible, on demand.
