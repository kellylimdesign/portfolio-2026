The problem

A New Kind of Actor

Twilio has identity tools for humans — but no way to safely authenticate or authorize the AI agents now acting alongside them. Imagine a financial tool, Owl Trade, whose users want to connect ChatGPT to trade on their behalf — how do you do that securely?
